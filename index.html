Rules

How to play

1

Choose a traveler. Glowing patches show its legal moves.

2

The tug and whale lead their crews. Surround the other leader to win.

3

Land on a non-leader traveler to capture it.

4

Dinghies and silver fish move toward the far shore. From home, they may move one or two clear patches.

5

A two-patch move can be caught in passing on the next turn.

6

At the far shore, a dinghy becomes a cutter; a silver fish becomes an orca. Play continues.

7

The tide clock ends long games, but any immediate reply still counts.

8

A safe leader with no legal move makes a draw.

9

Local matches have two games. Online rooms have two active players; others watch.
